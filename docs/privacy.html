Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-05-13.

Attic is a source-available Chrome extension that stores your saved-link data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any user data.

Data stored locally

The extension uses IndexedDB on your device to store:

  • URLs and titles of pages you have bookmarked in Chrome or captured through the extension.
  • Metadata fetched from those URLs, including Open Graph tags, favicons, and local text snapshots.
  • Counters for how often the extension has shown you each card.
  • Local state such as whether an item was marked as consumed or removed from Chrome bookmarks.

This data never leaves your device except for network requests to the exact URLs you saved, as described below.

Network requests

Attic fetches HTML from the exact URLs you have saved, solely to extract metadata such as title, description, image, favicon, and a local text snapshot. No data is sent to the extension's developers or to any third-party analytics server.

Permissions

  • bookmarks: read your Chrome bookmarks to populate the feed; move/delete the matching Chrome node when you edit tags or delete from Attic.
  • <all_urls> host permission: fetch metadata (title, description, image, favicon, text snapshot) from pages you saved.
  • storage and unlimitedStorage: store data locally on your device.
  • alarms: schedule background metadata fetches.
  • contextMenus and activeTab: support the toolbar popup, keyboard shortcut, and right-click capture menus. activeTab grants access only to the tab you're interacting with at the moment you invoke Attic.
  • notifications: when the capture mode preference is "Ask each time" (default), Chrome shows a notification with two buttons so you can pick Reading list or Bookmarks. Used solely for that prompt.
